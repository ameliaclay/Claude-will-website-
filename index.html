The value sits in a handful of boxes. Sell those one at a time to the right buyer, then move the common single LPs in a lot. Before listing anything, read the matrix number etched in the runout groove. That tiny code, not the title, is what separates a $10 copy from a $100 one.

01 Discogs

Best for the identifiable boxes and named-artist pressings. Serious collectors who grade by Goldmine standards and pay 10–15% more for classical and imports. 8% seller fee.

Best for · $30+ singles & boxes

02 eBay

Widest audience, and classical often moves faster here than on Discogs. Use auctions ending Sunday evening for the opera boxes. Roughly 13% final-value fee.

Best for · boxes, auctions, bundles

03 Local Austin shops

Waterloo Records, End of an Ear, Antone's and Breakaway buy collections for fast cash, but expect 25–40% of retail. Ideal for clearing the common LPs in one trip.

Best for · bulk, instant cash

04 Bundle the rest

Group the common sides by composer ("Five Handel pressings") or theme. Bundles sell slower but lift the per-record take and save you listing time.

Best for · the long tail
